Radar comes from the NOAA MRMS time-enabled image service; the selected CONUS frame is locked to its displayed UTC-derived time. WPC QPF supplies fixed 24-, 72- and 168-hour precipitation windows in inches of liquid equivalent. Radar echoes and forecast precipitation do not directly predict river inundation. Weather and reservoir source details.

Millerton/Friant and Pine Flat readings come from CDEC through a public-data collector. Collection targets 15-minute intervals; observations remain completed-day reports for Millerton and hourly reports for Pine Flat. River outlet release, spill and total outflow remain separate. Operator-reported schedules are shown separately with a seven-day look-ahead and a 24-hour handoff window. Source times and failed/overdue collection are shown; a schedule is not downstream arrival or proof of safe access.

Cameras come directly from the Caltrans District 6 camera catalog. Eight selected road cameras cover approaches to Fresno County river crossings. Boundary-side cameras retain their Madera or Tulare county label. Their view may not include the river. Still images refresh while visible; live video is on demand and may be unavailable. Images and video are not archived by this app. Caltrans conditions of use.

The 3D view uses Esri World Terrain with no vertical exaggeration. Ground detail varies by location. Gauges, camera locations and FEMA polygons are draped over terrain; they do not show water depth, levee reliability, camera visibility or safe access.

River watch uses NOAA NWPS gauge metadata and stage/flow series. Observation and forecast issuance times remain distinct. The 2-hour observation and 24-hour forecast age checks are prototype review rules, not NWS operating thresholds.

FEMA NFHL shows mapped 1% and 0.2% annual-chance areas and undetermined Zone D at local zoom levels. It is a planning reference, not a real-time inundation forecast. Other FEMA zones are filtered out. Read planning assumptions and next sources.

This prototype uses the official NWS watches, warnings and advisories layer. The publisher documents a five-minute update frequency. The app polls every five minutes while visible; browser timing may vary.

Each check intersects alert polygons with the five fixed county boundaries. Repeated polygons are combined using the NWS identifier and issuance time. Category colors simplify the map; original NWS product names remain in the list.

A failed or partial query retains the last successful result. Query time is not weather observation time. NWS ingestion time describes returned records, not an independent service heartbeat. Empty results never establish safe conditions.

Clearing a notification changes only this tab. It does not remove a hazard, record team review or close a task. No SMS, email or background escalation is enabled.

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau, 2026. Interface: Esri Calcite Design System. Source provenance.
