Official roads and source health

County roads reads road blocks, closures and detours through the county's existing public ArcGIS Online service connection. Its records were checked against the official public view. The app uses read-only queries and does not change either service. This connection lets the published app retrieve records without reaching directly into the county network. The app inventories ObjectIDs for complete retrieval but uses each layer and GlobalID for record identity. Source update, edit, start and end times remain separate. It queries every five minutes while open; temporary connection failures retry once. If a complete response cannot be verified, the last successful records remain visible with a failure notice and their original timestamps.

The default map shows records without elapsed end dates, excluding records explicitly marked inactive. Elapsed-date records with active flags are counted in a separate verification backlog and remain available through the history toggle. Include elapsed-date and inactive records exposes the rest of the returned history. Filtering is for readability; absence, inactivity or an elapsed end date never means a road is open. Contradictory flags and dates require verification. County field definitions and update responsibilities still need Road Maintenance confirmation.

The official evacuation map is a read-only reference. Its features do not provide individual update timestamps; the app does not assign an issuance time from a query timestamp. Field reports cannot modify either official source. The EOC queue separately identifies NWS alerts, planning review cues, verified conditions, overdue follow-ups, verification requests and feed failures.

Every table uses the same envelope: server ObjectID/GlobalID; unique event_id (80 characters); entity_id and incident_id (80); supersedes (4,000-character JSON list); payload (65,536-character JSON); server creator/creation and editor/edit dates. New events preserve earlier versions. Concurrent correction branches are shown as conflicts; they are not silently resolved by clock order. A retry reuses its event ID. Briefing content above the 60,000-character payload limit is rejected, never silently truncated.

Private PIO outreach

Open EOC workspace → PIO outreach, sign in to county AGOL and select an exercise incident. Coordinators save outreach and distribution records; the existing approvers group records documented county message approval. Group membership does not establish public-message authority. No new group membership or storage deployment is needed for the current owner’s test.

  1. Select a county evacuation zone and owner. Population and AFN/language evidence capture for new selections; wait for their results, including any unavailable-source notices. Existing evidence stays fixed until you use Refresh population evidence or Refresh AFN evidence. Record confirmed languages with the confirming person, time and evidence. Leave languages unconfirmed while gathering information.
  2. Enter one message language and text, planned channels, community partners and the next review time. Document county confirmation that the message applies to that zone. Census language categories do not confirm a person’s preferred language.
  3. Save the draft. An authorized approver records the external county approval and translation review for that exact version. A correction requires fresh approval. Before approval, the app checks the current county zone; changed or absent records require review.
  4. Record actual distribution through the approved channels, its evidence, receipt status and next update. This is a manual log; the app sends no alerts. Receipt remains unconfirmed unless a documented partner acknowledgment is recorded. Corrections preserve prior entries.
  5. Review overdue updates, delivery issues and changed zone applicability in the EOC queue. Use Create zone PIO handoff on an outreach record for that zone’s saved population, AFN/language, outreach, approval and receipt evidence; Create PIO handoff snapshot includes all saved PIO zones. These briefings use saved evidence and do not fetch current population or AFN values. Briefing approval does not independently approve a public message.

The source guide identifies the county Population Map and Population Density Map publishing account, COF_EOC_PROD, and the Census block publisher, esri_demographics. U.S. Census Bureau counts and county evacuation boundaries are inputs; the approximate zone allocation is a F.R.O.G. calculation. Each selected zone can save a newer ACS population allocation alongside the separate 2020 baseline. The snapshot retains its period, source query/edit dates, county zone revision, tract weights, method and uncertainty. AFN/language snapshots retain whole PUMA language estimates and 90% MOEs, whole-tract SVI/CalEnviroScreen context, and CDSS licensing records. Their own periods, query/edit dates, failures and whole-area limitations stay with each saved version. Language figures are not zone counts or confirmed language preferences; licensed capacity is not current occupants or transport demand. Cal OES communication guidance and the AFN Library provide further resources.

Recovery and maintenance

Keep private exports outside this public repository and outside site/. An authorized data owner can export each parent table to county-approved storage through AGOL Content; preserve the entire append history and editor-tracking fields, and record export time/item IDs. Confirm retention requirements with OES. The application itself provides printable/copyable versioned briefing handoffs, not a full database backup.

For public-map rollback, redeploy the compatible prior surface through the existing Pages workflow. Private PIO records introduced in 0.66 require the 0.66 EOC reader and validation modules; retain those modules when reverting the EOC interface. An earlier EOC reader does not recognize PIO approval records. Population evidence added in 0.68 needs the 0.68 reader to display its saved source dates and uncertainty. Retain the compatible PIO/population readers and writer when rolling back the private interface. AFN/language evidence added in 0.70 needs the compatible 0.70 PIO/AFN readers and writers. Reload open EOC tabs after deployment. Never delete or overwrite hosted exercise records as part of an app rollback. Keep the provisioning journal and AGOL item inventory in county-controlled storage. If a publication step is uncertain, rerun the publisher using the same journal; it resolves existing items before creating any dependency. If schema/sharing checks fail, inspect the existing item rather than republishing a duplicate.

Maintain separate public and private applications. No autonomous dispatch, activation, evacuation order, public messaging or state-system synchronization is implemented. FEMA, imagery and review areas remain context; none proves safe access or predicts inundation.
