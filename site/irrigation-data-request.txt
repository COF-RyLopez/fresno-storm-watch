FRESNO COUNTY F.R.O.G. — IRRIGATION NETWORK COORDINATION REQUEST
Prepared October 20, 2026

Subject: Read-only irrigation GIS and operator review for Fresno County storm planning

Hello GIS / Engineering / Water Operations team,

Fresno County is preparing its F.R.O.G. storm planning workspace for EOC
coordination. We would like to represent the documented connections between
river/reservoir supplies, canals, pipes, headgates, checks, turnouts, pumps,
interties, wasteways and recharge facilities in and connected to Fresno County.

Could you provide a current read-only GIS service or file geodatabase /
GeoPackage, a field and domain dictionary, and any related asset/link tables?
Please identify the data revision, horizontal CRS/datum and positional accuracy.

Requested attributes, where available and approved for sharing:
- Stable asset and node IDs, local names, type, owner and responsible operator.
- From-node / to-node links; flow direction, reversibility and seasonal or
  gate-dependent qualifications. Please identify crossings that do not connect.
- Active, replaced, abandoned, proposed and seasonal inventory classifications.
- Capacity, units, basis, date and operator confirmation; distinguish design,
  observed and currently available capacity. Unknown values should remain null.
- A read-only status/measurement source, observation time, timezone, units,
  quality flag, expected update interval and maintenance contact.
- Approved power-feed / backup-power dependencies and readiness confirmation,
  if these may be shared with authorized county emergency staff.
- Intake and outlet links to rivers, reservoirs, district boundaries and
  neighboring systems, with a short operator review of the important junctions.

Please specify separately what may be displayed publicly, what is county-only
or partner-only, and any required attribution, license or written data-sharing
agreement. We can keep operational assets in private AGOL services. We are not
requesting control-system access or write access to your operational systems.

For Fresno Irrigation District specifically, we found the public "Facilities
April 2018" archive (ArcGIS item 84307198681c46ab9e10acfa862f7606). Its embedded
metadata says "Contact FID for authorization." Please confirm permitted use and
provide a current replacement, including separate gate/pump/control structures
and relationship tables if available. We have not republished that archive.

For FMFCD, we found the public FID Facilities layer with 5,600 line records.
Its item terms require written consent for publication/distribution. Please
confirm permission, source date and whether a current richer schema is available.

Our initial purpose is situational awareness and verification before resource
planning. A mapped connection will not be treated as present flow, gate position,
capacity, flood arrival time or authorization to operate a facility.

Suggested coordination order:
1. FID, Consolidated Irrigation District in Selma, Alta, Laguna, KRCD / KRWA.
2. James, Tranquillity, CCID, Firebaugh Canal Water District, other Exchange
   Contractors and relevant Mendota / river operators.
3. Westlands, SLDMWA, Friant Water Authority, Reclamation, and remaining county
   irrigation / water districts and private ditch companies.

Please nominate a GIS contact and an operations reviewer for a short network
walkthrough. We can return a draft map of the supplied data for your corrections.

Thank you.
