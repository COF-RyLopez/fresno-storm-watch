Source and test method

    Both methods use identical recorded WPC responses and image bytes. The prepared route loads a manifest and selected image instead of rebuilding the forecast through service queries. These are display images, not numerical depth grids or a new weather model. The published comparison replays static responses; it does not benchmark live NWS service latency.

    Measurements end when validated weather data is ready. They exclude terrain startup, GPU painting, interactive sign-in and production CDN performance. No private data or persistent browser cache is used.
