F.R.O.G. UI / UX ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
Created 2026-10-28 • Initial audit baseline 0.58.0 • Corrections 0.58.1

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Check both whether each control works and whether an EOC operator can complete
a useful task without coaching. Automated tests, a browser smoke test, staff
usability testing, server authorization testing and hydraulic validation are
different activities. Passing one does not establish the others.

For every case record: release/commit, browser/device/viewport, account role,
exercise/live source mode, starting state, exact actions, expected result,
actual result, PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT RUN, evidence and retest release.
An opened screen is a smoke-test pass, not a pass for every control on it.

TEST ORDER
1. Inventory screens and controls, including runtime-generated record actions.
2. Test the core public navigation and map/evidence interactions.
3. Test valid, empty, failed, stale, loading and interrupted states.
4. Test private workflows with viewer and authorized editing accounts, using
   clearly separate private exercise records. Do not edit live incident data.
5. Check keyboard, focus, forms, contrast, text enlargement and real phone use.
6. Observe non-specialist EOC staff completing mission tasks without guidance.
7. Fix failures, add meaningful regression checks and retest affected workflows.

SCREEN AND CONTROL COVERAGE
G01 Header: County Watch / Planning / Briefing, Topic tools, Account & help,
    theme, help and EOC links. Check Back/Forward, reload and deep links.
G02 Monitoring strip: each shortcut reaches its intended evidence. Distinguish
    count units and scope: areas, care records, utility records, regional alerts,
    model comparisons and source checks are not interchangeable incident totals.
G03 Evidence: selection zoom/highlight; close / Escape / focus return; replace
    obsolete evidence on topic changes. Preserve a working map during transitions.
G04 Map: Fit, Expand/Restore, search, layers, basemap gallery, 2D/3D and cameras.
    Check resize, rapid switching, camera state, failed 3D and restoration.
G05 Search: a lake outline, stream, road and authorized asset; no matches and
    unavailable sources. Verify full-outline zoom, highlight, clear, nearby
    records, distance units and the limits of proximity versus network access.

W01 County Watch: each concern type, filter, pagination, selection and empty
    results. Countywide counts do not change with map panning or display filters.
W02 Follow-up: choose incident, owner/team, due time, required verification and
    status. Confirm saved updates are visible again, without duplicate tasks.
W03 Care: county zones, utility areas, nearby roads and source gaps; distinguish
    a care record, occupancy, building footprint and verified facility conditions.
W04 Pumps: inventory identity, readiness, fuel/runtime, owner contact, assignment,
    overdue/change/rematch states and rainfall accumulation windows versus onset.

T01 Rivers: watchpoint/group/window filters, observations versus forecasts, units,
    unknown thresholds, peak times, source links and unavailable point forecasts.
T02 Weather: radar/GOES/rainfall exclusivity; Now/Earlier/Later; shared time;
    HRRR and NWS comparison; issue/valid windows and legends. Future times must
    not display observations as forecasts. Briefing-tour windows remain explicit.
T03 Briefing tour: previous/next slide and layer, sync on/off, unavailable source,
    legend colors, product dates, source links and ending the tour.
T04 Reservoirs: Millerton/Pine Flat selection, release trends, downstream versus
    upstream gauges, schedules, observation dates and unavailable metrics.
T05 Alerts: regional scope, category, selected polygon, official source, revision,
    expiration and repeated polygons. Acknowledgement does not clear a hazard.
T06 Power: current/PSPS/unknown/history filters, utility details, circuit matches,
    unverified connections, nearby assets, automatic building display, unknown
    heights and associated addresses. Never infer a fault or site service.
T07 Roads: block/closure/detour selection, inactive/history toggle, elapsed dates,
    active flag contradictions, missing records, unknown geometry and official
    links. A past end date or disappeared record does not establish reopening.
T08 Drainage: sign-in, existing/proposed separation, basin/pump selection, network
    direction and unverified links; rainfall/road/facility/power context. Test
    direct readiness form and inventory freshness without changing live sources.

P01 Planning: selected scenario versus the map actually loaded, loading/failure,
    repeat selection, clear, independent clock, play/pause, keyboard slider and
    3D. Keep hypothetical and published source assumptions conspicuous.
P02 Advanced runs: no enabled template / no approved host / coverage gaps must
    not suggest a submitted or computed scenario. Library playback is not a run.
B01 Briefing: capture, repeated capture, remove, title, empty state, copy/download,
    private incident/period, save, approval handoff and exact version references.
B02 Unsaved draft: navigate, refresh, close, sign-out and interrupted save. Do not
    persist private drafts in browser storage. Warn before accidental loss.

E01 Private EOC: Overview, Review queue, Field conditions, Follow-ups & decisions,
    Readiness & staging, Briefing & handoff, and Record history; all display roles.
E02 Reports: required fields, invalid dates/coordinates/URLs, save, correction and
    revision-specific verification. Reports start unverified; official layers stay
    separate. Show useful retry feedback without duplicating saved records.
E03 Incident/period: authorized activation record, authority, effective time,
    period and next briefing. Display-role choice must never grant permission.
E04 Private access: anonymous/viewer/reporter/reviewer/activation-recorder tests
    against server queries and writes, not merely hidden buttons. Include expired
    sign-in, revoked membership, logout and late responses clearing private data.
E05 Briefing/state handoff: approve/withdraw exact snapshots; copy/print draft and
    approved versions; external references. Export never means submitted to state.

A01 Keyboard-only: complete a core task; visible focus; meaningful tab order;
    dialog/pane focus entry, Escape and return; accessible names and form errors.
A02 Readability: 1440x900, 1280x720, phone portrait and 200% text/zoom in county
    Chrome/Edge. Check legends, units, clipping, nested scrolling and touch use.
A03 Check contrast and non-color status meaning; screen-reader labels; reduced
    motion, pause controls and focus stability during background refresh.
F01 Feed failures: fail one feed while others remain available; stale retained
    records, missing coverage and unassessed sources must not become zero/safe.
F02 Longer session: keep the app open through refreshes, source revisions,
    network interruption, sleep/wake and a shift handoff; observe responsiveness.

STAFF USABILITY SESSION (proposed, not county policy)
Use at least two non-specialist EOC testers, plus an experienced reviewer. Give
tasks rather than click instructions. Record time, wrong turns, assistance and
what the user believes the evidence means. Test with workstation and phone.
1. Find a place needing review and explain the reason, timing and uncertainty.
2. Inspect roads, power and facilities without confusing overlap with confirmed
   impact or proximity with a usable route.
3. Assign a verification follow-up and find its owner/due/status again.
4. Load a published hypothetical scenario and correctly identify its assumptions.
5. Prepare a handoff with sources, outstanding gaps and unresolved work.
6. In the later recovery/closeout increment, verify restoration and close work
   without clearing an agency hazard or implying an official reopening.

RELEASE ACCEPTANCE
Resolve defects that misidentify displayed evidence, lose private work silently,
permit unauthorized access/writes, or confuse hypothetical/forecast/live status.
No blanket PASS until private account and staff tests are complete. An initial
accessibility check is not a WCAG conformance certification. Numerical model
validation remains with the appropriate technical reviewers/model owners.
