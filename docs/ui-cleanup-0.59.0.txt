F.R.O.G. — STABILITY AND USABILITY INCREMENT 0.59.0
2026-10-28

WHAT CHANGED
Source health separates failed requests, stale/overdue data, coverage gaps,
unverified times, first checks, unassessed references and current checks.
Review totals count the first four categories by underlying source key. A recent
app check does not renew an old observation/forecast. Optional network references
and signed-out private records remain explicitly unassessed. Categories are
expandable and retain their open/closed state across updates.

Weather puts the shared timeline and atmospheric overlay ahead of specialist
products. NWS wind, HRRR, comparisons and the briefing tour remain available in
collapsed sections. Existing legends, source/valid times, data collectors,
observation-versus-forecast rules and hazard thresholds remain in effect.

Road cards have one named review action. Verification history and contradictory
dates remain available. Unknown or elapsed dates do not establish reopening.

Briefing captures ignore identical repeated evidence, while retaining changed
versions. Empty actions and signed-out private saving have explicit prerequisites.
Unsaved revisions install a native page-exit warning; native warning availability
varies by browser/device. No draft is stored persistently in the browser.
Private-save callbacks retain their selected incident, period, title and evidence
revision across asynchronous requests. Period choices expose dates and elapsed
windows, omit conflicting/superseded revisions, and prefer a current recorded
window when available. Approval remains separate from saving/exporting.

County Watch has an expandable private incident-follow-up section. Choose an
exercise incident to load its saved general assignments; checks repeat each minute
while the page is visible. Owner, due time and status are shown; completed/canceled
records are optional history. Overdue tasks add a separate labeled cue to the
Area reviews shortcut. They do not increase the area count or clear hazards.
Only exact stored evidence identities link tasks to concerns; unlinked legacy
entries and conflicting corrections remain explicit. Captures freeze task event
versions in briefing evidence. Pump readiness/assignment scopes remain separate.

Private query failures and sign-out clear follow-up records and counts. Epoch
checks reject late results. Named-account permissions are still enforced by AGOL;
no groups, sharing or hosted schemas were changed. EOC follow-up cards emphasize
status, owner, due time and requested review; stored evidence is expandable.
New form defaults include only relevant fields, and empty history is explained.
