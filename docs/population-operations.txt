Checked October 28, 2026.

County Watch -> Population & evacuation zones -> select a county zone.
Read-only example: /?view=watch&population=1&zone=L62&v=0.67.0

Newer source totals: Living Atlas ACS Population view, published by
esri_demographics, Current Vintage 2020-2024 checked from item metadata.
https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=60c98f20a162416ea1725b94d7297f83
Use B01001_001E total residents and B01001_001M published 90% MOE.
This is a five-year survey estimate, not 2026 population or live occupancy.

Analysis geometry: Census TIGERweb full Census Tracts, January 1, 2024 vintage.
https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/tigerWMS_ACS2024/MapServer/8
The matching period is pinned to 2020-2024. An annual ACS period change fails
this allocation explicitly until matching Census analysis boundaries are configured.
Full tract boundaries are read from Census with STATE='06', COUNTY='019',
positive-area intersection with the selected current county evacuation zone,
reconciled object-ID batches, WGS84 output and no simplification.
Living Atlas water-erased display outlines are not used for calculation.
https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/geography-acs/geography-boundaries-by-year.2024.html

Allocation method (F.R.O.G. implementation, not a county/Census zone product):
For each intersecting full tract T and the selected zone Z, calculate:
  denominator = sum(P2020_block * area(block intersect T) / area(block))
  numerator   = sum(P2020_block * area(block intersect T intersect Z) / area(block))
  weight      = numerator / denominator
  contribution = weight * ACS_tract_total
Sum the unrounded contributions, rounding only the displayed result.
A wholly contained tract uses its whole published ACS total with weight 1.
All intersections and areas use the ArcGIS SDK operators in geodetic square metres;
tracts/blocks retain holes and multipart geometry. Reject overlapping tract coverage.
This also handles block-to-tract boundary differences without assuming GEOID nesting.

The 2020 block source and complete ID reconciliation remain unchanged. Each
partial tract queries its full block population pattern, not just zone-cut blocks.
Unknown or unpopulated patterns in a partial overlap produce unknown allocation,
not a current zero or inferred new development. Missing ACS totals prevent a
complete estimate. Never sum whole intersecting tracts as zone totals.
No building, parcel, address or individual demographic assignment is performed.

Uncertainty:
Weights assume the within-tract distribution has remained like 2020; new growth,
group-quarters changes, rural block uniformity, water/boundary differences and
Census privacy protection can materially affect results. Newer is not necessarily
more spatially accurate. Keep the separate 2020 block estimate, not a growth delta.
No zone-level age, language, disability or household allocation is made; those
remain whole-tract context and do not establish individual communication needs.

The expandable evidence retains whole-tract estimates/MOEs and each allocation
weight. The optional sampling-only component is sqrt(sum((weight * tract_MOE)^2)).
It treats weights as fixed and ignores tract covariance; it excludes all allocation,
boundary, Census and population-change errors. Do not label it a full zone 90%
confidence interval, fabricate confidence bounds or attach it to the 2020 baseline.
https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2020/acs/acs_general_handbook_2020_ch08.pdf
No overlap, missing data and failed sources never become confirmed zero/safety.
Overlapping zone totals must not be added; no countywide affected total is claimed.

Runtime:
Public query-only requests, credentials omitted, bounded 25/30-second requests.
No new AGOL items, permissions, API keys, dependencies, scheduled jobs or credits.
ACS current period/item modification/layer edit markers are rechecked after math;
changed metadata discards the result. Full boundary IDs are reconciled twice.
Bound: 500 source tracts, 25,000 blocks/request and 75,000 block records of work.
Geometry yields every 25 blocks. Aborted or superseded selections cannot repaint.
Newer calculation runs independently of the 2020 baseline. Partial source failure
retains the available counterpart with distinct timestamps and failure notice.
At most five newer zone results / eight tract block collections cached for ten
minutes in memory; zone geometry changes and Retry invalidate the relevant caches.
No public or private operational data is written to persistent browser storage.

Acceptance: focused allocation/source tests plus regression suite and check.
Verify three live county zones; compare contributing ACS records with publisher
values; exercise rapid switching and inspect the evidence table for overflow.
No actual evacuations, current occupants or population growth are inferred.
Rollback: redeploy 0.66.0. No data migration; compatible PIO private reader retained.

Calculation:
Fetch every intersecting block using reconciled ID batches, WGS84 polygon output,
and no geometry simplification. Validate county GEOIDs, population and geometry.
Use SDK intersectionOperator and geodeticAreaOperator in geodetic square metres.
Estimate = sum(block population * overlap polygon area / whole block polygon area).
This assumes uniform population across each complete boundary block, including
water. It is particularly uncertain in large rural blocks. No land mask, building
allocation, households or individual location inference is performed.
Show fully contained block population and the full population of boundary-cut
blocks separately; the latter is not added to the estimate. The resulting context
is not a statistical confidence interval. Census privacy protections also affect
small-area counts. All labels retain the 2020 reference date.
Missing/suppressed population prevents a complete estimate; no overlap is unknown,
not zero. Touch-only intersections have zero weight. GEOIDs are counted once.
Never add zone totals together: zones may overlap. Current version assesses one
zone at a time and does not claim a countywide affected population.

Runtime and safeguards:
No new dependencies, collectors, credentials or backend. Census queries use public
POST form queries with credentials omitted; POST is retrieval only. Each request
has a 30-second deadline and reconciles returned IDs. A 25,000-block ceiling fails
explicitly. Calculation yields after each 25 records. Selection epochs and abort
signals suppress superseded work; closing evidence cancels incomplete calculation.
At most five geometry-keyed results are cached for ten minutes in memory only.
Zone source checks repeat every five minutes while the population section is open.
Failures retain an explicit unknown/failure state; a changed boundary requires a
new calculation. No population records or private records enter browser persistence.
Private feature layers, authoring tools, activation and state submissions remain separate.
