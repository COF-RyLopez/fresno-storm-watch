These views currently report 2020-2024, not a 2026 population estimate. Runtime
reads the publisher's Current Vintage, validates fields/owner, and displays that
period. Cached metadata expires after 15 minutes. PIO topics with a different
period fail explicitly rather than silently combining periods. Failed individual
topics do not erase available topics. No geometry or data is persisted in browser
storage and no new hosted items, permissions or credentials are introduced.

Population fields B01001_001E/M, B01001_calc_numLT18E/M and
B01001_calc_numGE65E/M supply total, under-18 and age-65+ estimates and 90% MOEs.
A missing/negative/suppressed value remains unavailable; an explicit zero stays
zero. MOEs may exceed estimates. They describe sampling uncertainty, not all error.
No MOE is calculated for the separate approximate 2020 block/zone allocation.
Density = total tract residents / (ALAND square metres / 2589988.110336).
The map uses fixed sequential classes, a gray missing-data symbol and land-area
units. The publisher's water-trimmed outlines are cartographic context, never the
basis of a zone population calculation. Pink outline means selected whole tract;
cyan remains the selected county zone. Neither is a hazard boundary.

PIO context is on demand, not another default overlay or tab:
- C16001: people age 5+ speaking English less than very well, including publisher
  language groups. Keep broad categories intact; do not infer Hmong or Punjabi
  counts, individual preference or ethnicity. Values are publisher-provided; no
  local sum of language groups or new derived MOE is made.
- B28002_013E/M: households without internet; B28002_006E/M: cellular-only
  subscription. These are households, not people, coverage or delivery metrics.
- B18101_calc_numDE/DM: civilian noninstitutionalized residents with disability;
  institutional care residents are excluded. This is not a registry of individuals
  needing assistance and does not replace resident-care facility information.
- B08201_002E/M: households with no vehicle available; not requested bus seats or
  a routing/accessibility analysis. Use owner-confirmed transport arrangements.
Do not add these overlapping groups or mix people with households.

Calculation:
Fetch every intersecting block using reconciled ID batches, WGS84 polygon output,
and no geometry simplification. Validate county GEOIDs, population and geometry.
Use SDK intersectionOperator and geodeticAreaOperator in geodetic square metres.
Estimate = sum(block population * overlap polygon area / whole block polygon area).
This assumes uniform population across each complete boundary block, including
water. It is particularly uncertain in large rural blocks. No land mask, building
allocation, households or individual location inference is performed.
Show fully contained block population and the full population of boundary-cut
blocks separately; the latter is not added to the estimate. The resulting context
is not a statistical confidence interval. Census privacy protections also affect
small-area counts. All labels retain the 2020 reference date.
Missing/suppressed population prevents a complete estimate; no overlap is unknown,
not zero. Touch-only intersections have zero weight. GEOIDs are counted once.
Never add zone totals together: zones may overlap. Current version assesses one
zone at a time and does not claim a countywide affected population.

Runtime and safeguards:
No new dependencies, collectors, credentials or backend. Census queries use public
POST form queries with credentials omitted; POST is retrieval only. Each request
has a 30-second deadline and reconciles returned IDs. A 25,000-block ceiling fails
explicitly. Calculation yields after each 25 records. Selection epochs and abort
signals suppress superseded work; closing evidence cancels incomplete calculation.
At most five geometry-keyed results are cached for ten minutes in memory only.
Zone source checks repeat every five minutes while the population section is open.
Failures retain an explicit unknown/failure state; a changed boundary requires a
new calculation. No population records or private records enter browser persistence.
Private feature layers, authoring tools, activation and state submissions remain separate.
