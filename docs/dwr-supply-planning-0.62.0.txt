F.R.O.G. 0.62.0 — DWR supply context in County / Area Watch

Open County Watch > DWR supply planning, or ?view=watch&dwrSupplies=1.
The section is collapsed by default; it adds no risk score or incident count.
Live public DWR inventory queries are scoped to Fresno County using the existing
county/object-ID reconciliation. No AGOL items, permissions or source records
are changed. Public resource records remain in tab memory only.

Association rules
- Default 25 km straight-line point proximity; 10 and 50 km alternatives.
- Associate only existing gauge/dam watchpoints from the six river review groups.
- These are NOT supply service areas, routed river travel times, allocations,
  verified access or staging approvals. No inferred reach boundaries or routes.
- Road geometry is compared to the supply point and the associated watchpoints
  separately, within 5 km; the evidence says which location each distance uses.
- Source-inactive records stay in Roads history. Elapsed end dates on active or
  uncertain records remain for verification, never automatically reopened.
- Deduplicate stable road keys; exclude conflicting revisions and bad geometry
  with coverage notices. Source errors / >7 minute query age require verification.
- WPC amounts and forecast validity come from existing usable point checks;
  unknown/stale values remain unknown. Values are not basin means or runoff.

Inspection / availability
Display source inspection UTC calendar date and calendar age, separately from
the live query check time. Historical, unknown and future dates are qualified.
No arbitrary DWR readiness age threshold is introduced. Stock availability,
allocation and pickup authorization remain unverified for every record.
At onboarding the two Fresno records were inspected Oct 1 and Sep 25, 2024.
Reported zeros stay zero; missing quantities remain unknown.

Map / refresh
Inspect supply context opens the existing right evidence surface and fits the
selected supply plus its associated watchpoints. Hollow white circle = selected
supply; hollow cyan = nearby watchpoints. DWR original container symbols and
county road status meanings remain intact. No line suggesting a route is drawn.
The selected context also draws nearby elapsed/uncertain road records, retaining
their full source geometry and click-through identity, without changing the
global Roads history filter. These reference records still require verification.
Supply queries refresh every five minutes while the page is visible, independent
of map layer visibility once monitoring is requested. Refresh cannot re-enable a
hidden layer. Failures clear current inventory and appear in source health.
Inventory browsing is disabled in the fictional exercise view.

Wind graphic clarification
Weather > HRRR animated winds & gusts > Fit HRRR coverage. Animated 10 m wind
forecast trails and gust shading use NOAA HRRR prepared through Dynamical.
The shared timeline chooses forecast valid time. Prepared snapshot files refresh
through the existing Pages collector; they are not live wind observations.
Trails are illustrative animation, not measured storm travel speed. Existing
run/valid/collection times, freshness gates, NWS forecasts and pause controls
are retained. No weather collector or rendering logic changed in this release.

Validation
Nine new model tests cover source identity/CRS, calendar ages, bounded watchpoint
links, full road geometry, stale/error handling, elapsed status, duplicate and
conflicting records, filters and known Fresno associations. Full regression and
deployment checks plus browser verification are recorded in the release output.
Rollback: redeploy 0.61.0. No data migration or ArcGIS permission changes.
