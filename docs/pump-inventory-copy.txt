Refresh (manual pilot, no scheduled synchronization)
1. Open https://cof-rylopez.github.io/fresno-storm-watch/pump-inventory.html
2. Sign in as the existing county exercise owner.
3. Choose Read FMFCD source. Only the maintainer may need Enterprise sign-in.
4. Review counts/classifications and the source-check time.
5. Choose Create / refresh private AGOL copy. Keep the page open until Verified.
The maintainer stages an append-only generation, reads every row back, verifies
its digest/count/identity, then updates the read view's server-side definition.
The last verified view remains active if staging fails. Retry uses the prepared
generation/unique row keys, not another copy of each pump. Do not recreate items
when a response is uncertain. Inspect existing owner items first.
Older source checks cannot replace a newer active copy. Avoid simultaneous
maintainers; this small manual pilot does not provide a distributed refresh lock.

Freshness
The form shows FMFCD source-check time separately from its AGOL retrieval time.
AGOL retrieval must be within ten minutes; the source copy must be within seven
days. Seven days is a configurable pilot inventory policy, not pump readiness or
a hazard threshold. Refresh after source edits and at least weekly during use.
Stale source copies block new confirmations; they never imply safe operation.
For an unattended refresh, county IT must approve a scheduler, source identity,
secret storage, failure notification and maintenance owner. None is configured.

Security and verification
Named AGOL accounts, server-enforced private sharing, and Query-only view access
protect inventory. The original readiness storage still enforces editing rights
and author/time. The maintenance page checks the exact owner/org, while AGOL
enforces item ownership. No credentials, inventory features or contacts are
published to GitHub/Pages or persistent browser storage. Source snapshots retain
provenance and corrections; retention/purge policy remains a county GIS task.
Verify denied anonymous queries, Query-only view permissions and two different
participant accounts before expanding the private exercise pilot.

Rollback
Redeploy the previous Pages release (0.46.0) to return the form to its original
Enterprise inventory path. Keep copied items private; do not delete saved
readiness history. A source snapshot can be reselected only after verifying its
counts/digest and choosing its exact snapshot ID in the view definition. Never
remove the existing-only filter or substitute an unverified source generation.
