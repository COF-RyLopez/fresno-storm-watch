   Run focused tests, regression tests, deployment checks and browser checks for

   Publish only after these checks; verify the published page. Rollback: redeploy

6. INCIDENT WORK FOCUS [next increment]
   Add Prepare / Respond / Recover / Closeout as a view of work for the selected
   private incident, not an activation control. Prepare shows timing/readiness;
   Respond shows verified changes, overdue follow-ups and decisions; Recover
   shows inspections and restoration verification; Closeout shows unresolved
   assignments, approvals, exports and lessons. County OES confirms terminology,
   closure authority and required forms before any new official-state workflow.
   Reuse private coordination, incident, period and briefing records; preserve
   append-only corrections and server permissions. Do not clear source hazards
   when a task or incident is closed.

7. SCENARIO EVIDENCE QUALITY [parallel research / next increment]
   Reconcile each library source/version, original numeric grids, units/datums,
   arrival threshold and permitted use. Distinguish published maximum/arrival
   products from interpolated frames. Before the one-foot arrival threshold,
   conditions are not established as dry. Keep illustrative evacuation traffic,
   population and economic-loss calculations outside the default command center.
   Compare facilities/roads with source geometry and retained uncertainties; NSI
   occupancy records must not be counted as unique physical building footprints.
   Seek technical review from model owners. Existing software tests alone do not
   establish engineering validity. Custom hydraulic runs remain separately gated.

8. TABLETOP AND ITERATION [county participation needed]
   With two EOC testers, exercise: identify concern → inspect timing/gaps → assign
   verification → capture briefing → shift handoff → verify restoration → closeout.
   Record completion time, missed information and navigation errors. Verify a
   viewer cannot write and an authorized account can complete its permitted work.
   Address observed friction before adding feeds or more navigation.
