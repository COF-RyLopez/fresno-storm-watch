F.R.O.G. 0.69.0 — AFN & language context for a selected county evacuation zone

Where: Population & evacuation zones > choose a zone > "AFN & language context · whole areas".
Loads on first open. Anonymous, read-only public queries; nothing is stored or written.
Read-only example: /?view=watch&population=1&zone=L39&v=0.69.0

What each number comes from
- Hmong and Punjabi speakers, and those speaking English less than "very well": ACS 2020–2024
  table B16001 (lines 054/056 Punjabi, 084/086 Hmong, 001 age 5+), with 90% MOEs. The Census Bureau
  publishes this table for PUMAs only (7 in Fresno County, ~100k+ residents each), not tracts.
  Stored in site/data/acs-b16001-fresno.json by `npm run collect:pio-languages [year]` from the public
  summary file (no API key). PUMAs intersecting the zone come from TIGERweb ACS2024 layer 0.
- CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 (2020 tracts, ACS 2018–2022): overall and household-characteristics national
  percentiles, limited-English persons ± MOE. Summary = tract count, tracts at or above the 75th
  percentile, highest value. No averaging.
- CalEnviroScreen 4.0 percentile and linguistic-isolation percentile (OEHHA, 2010 tracts) with the
  SB 535 2022 designation (CalEPA, tribal update 2024). 2010 tracts are matched to the zone by spatial
  overlap only, never by GEOID against 2020 tracts.
- CDSS Community Care Licensing, PROGRAM_TYPE 'ADULT AND SENIOR' (elderly and adult residential,
  adult day care, ARFPSHN, social rehabilitation). Children's residential, resource family homes and
  child care are not requested. Phone, licensee and street address are never requested.
  CDSS STATUS is an undocumented numeric code (Fresno 2026-10-28: 3 most records, also 4 and 6);
  records and capacity are grouped by code and never added across codes. Capacity is licensed
  capacity, not residents or transport need. CDPH skilled nursing stays in Resident care.

Limits
Every value describes a whole PUMA or tract that intersects the zone, including tracts that only
touch it. Nothing is allocated to the zone, buildings or people. Sources differ in period and
geography; do not add, average or compare across them. A source failure shows "Unavailable" for that
topic only. No message is sent, translated or approved here; the PIO/JIC workflow is unchanged.

Recovery and selection lifecycle (0.69.0)
- If any topic fails, successful topics stay visible and "Retry AFN sources" checks again. Closing
  and reopening the section retries failed topics too. Repeated clicks share the pending request.
- "Retry selected zone" refreshes the AFN context along with the population calculation.
- Changing/clearing a zone, closing its evidence, leaving County Watch or leaving the page cancels
  the old AFN request. Selection/disposal guards also reject late responses when a transport ignores
  cancellation. Old responses cannot replace the selected zone's source-health check.
- Ordinary calculation/source re-renders retain the open context for an unchanged zone boundary.
- All context remains read-only and in memory; no additional accounts, storage or permissions.
- Six regression tests cover partial/total failure recovery, duplicate requests, disposal, selection
  guards and cancellation propagation. Existing population/PIO snapshot tests are retained.
- Released as 0.69.0 because the private PIO population-evidence release already uses 0.68.0.
