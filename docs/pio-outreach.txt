F.R.O.G. 0.68.0 — Saved zone population evidence in private outreach and briefings
Checked October 28, 2026. Existing private EXERCISE AGOL tables and permissions.

Open eoc.html?v=0.68.0#outreach, sign in, select an incident, then create/update
outreach. A new zone selection, or an older record without evidence, calculates
public population evidence. Previously saved evidence is retained until Refresh
population evidence is used. Review the result and save the outreach version.
No private message, language, contact or partner values are sent to publishers.

Optional pioOutreach.populationEvidence schema 1, method frog-zone-population/1:
- County zone source/key/OID/name/status/query time and exact representation revision.
- Newer ACS total allocation, raw estimates, sampling-only MOE, missing counts,
  per-tract population/MOE/weight, baseline pattern and block query/edit dates.
- Separate 2020 block-overlap baseline, block accounting and source query/edit dates.
- ACS five-year period, publisher item modification/layer edit times, query time;
  full Census tract analysis-boundary vintage/query time and authoritative URLs.
- Capture/calculation times, original method/uncertainty/limitation wording and failures.
No polygons, whole source collections, buildings or household assignments are stored.
The 0.67 method is unchanged. Source references remain those documented in
population-operations.txt. Full numeric values are retained; rounding is display only.

Missing/failed source results stay unknown with saved failure evidence. A partial
source failure preserves the available counterpart. Unpopulated 2020 patterns do
not become zero current population. A changed county revision is flagged rather
than silently applying a historical estimate to a new boundary/status. Counts are
planning residents, not confirmed occupants, evacuees, language needs or demand.
The sampling component is not a full zone 90% confidence interval. Age/language
figures remain whole-tract context. Query/capture times are not new observations.

Refresh/save appends a draft version. It requires fresh exact-version message
approval; previous messages, approvals, receipts and evidence remain in history.
No population calculation approves or sends public messaging. Closed test history
can retain its new evidence without being reopened or generating a new task.

Create zone PIO handoff freezes only the selected zone's PIO histories, approvals,
manual receipts, relevant review cues and population evidence. Unrelated zones and
live source-health rows are excluded; population source failures are already in the
saved evidence. Generic PIO/all briefings also carry population evidence. Briefing
text uses only the frozen data and original method text; opening or printing it
never fetches the latest counts. The existing briefing SHA-256 pins that snapshot.
A later source update or refreshed outreach does not alter an earlier briefing.

No new items, hosted fields, groups, OAuth scope, credentials or memberships.
Named coordinators edit existing coordination rows; existing approvers record
message/briefing decisions; private read-only views retain server-enforced access.
No private inputs/results are written to GitHub, deployment files, URLs or browser
persistence. Abort/incident/session guards clear incomplete captures and private forms.
Bounds: snapshot 45,000 characters; entire private event and briefing 60,000. Very
large histories fail visibly; evidence is never truncated to force a successful save.
Rollback needs compatible 0.68 population readers and writers to retain/display new
optional evidence. Old records without population evidence remain readable.
Reload open EOC tabs after deployment. Exercise-only synthetic counts are explicitly
labeled illustrative; actual AGOL acceptance uses public-source counts for L62.

F.R.O.G. 0.66.0 — Private PIO outreach and population-source guidance

Entry: eoc.html?v=0.66.0#outreach -> county sign-in -> select an exercise incident

Workflow
1. Select a county evacuation-zone record; enter purpose, responsible PIO/team,
   confirmed languages and their confirming person/time/evidence. Unconfirmed
   languages can remain blank in a draft. One message language/text per version.
2. Record channels, community partners, next update/review and county confirmation
   of message applicability. Saving creates an append-only coordination event.
3. An account with existing approval access records documented county PIO/JIC
   approval for that exact event. Record the actual decision maker, decision time
   and evidence. Group membership is not public-message authority. Before approval
   the app rechecks source representation; a changed or missing zone requires
   review. A zone query older than 15 minutes requires a refreshed draft version.
4. Record actual external distribution, channels/partners used, receipt status,
   supporting evidence and next update. Sent/posted is explicitly receipt
   unconfirmed. Partner acknowledgment requires the operator's evidence. The app
   does not send, post, translate or approve public release by itself.
5. Correct a plan, approval or receipt with another append. Plan revisions require
   fresh exact-version approval; a receipt never edits the approved message.
   Historical receipt corrections keep their original message and approval links.
6. Create PIO handoff snapshot freezes PIO coordination/history, exact approvals,
   manual receipts, operational period and current PIO review evidence. Unrelated
   county feed records are excluded, avoiding the generic full-briefing size limit.
   The existing 60,000-character record bound still applies to large histories.
   A PIO handoff remains a draft until separately approved as a briefing. Briefing
   approval does not independently approve its public messages. Exports are manual
   handoffs, not submissions to Cal OES.
7. Close or cancel with evidence. Prior versions remain available in Record
   history and saved briefing snapshots. Closing does not clear the hazard.

Storage and access
No new hosted items, physical fields, group memberships or permissions.
- coordination payload.pioOutreach: coordinator write view; owner, title,
  follow-up status, due time, source-bound zone snapshot, language confirmation,
  message language/text, channels, partners, applicability confirmation and notes.
- approvals payload.kind=pio-outreach: existing approver write view; exact
  outreachEvent, Approved/Withdrawn, decisionBy, decidedAt and evidence.
- coordination payload.pioDistribution: coordinator write view; exact outreach
  event/entity and approval event, actual time, channels/partners used, receipt
  status, evidence and next update. Earlier receipt events are superseded, not
  overwritten. Author and entry time come from server editor tracking.
Existing viewer read views remain query-only. Anonymous access was denied for
incident, coordination and approval read/write views. New domain validation checks
the freshly loaded incident, current heads and exact approval links. Direct API
domain rules remain client-validated; AGOL enforces access and append-only views.
Derivations do not treat a receipt without a matching approval as verified.

Private content stays in authenticated AGOL and tab memory, including unfinished
forms. No private data, drafts, contacts or message text is written to GitHub,
deploy artifacts, URL parameters or persistent browser storage. Sign-out, private
query failure, incident changes and page exit clear the editor and private state.
Keep any owner exports in county-approved storage outside this repository.

Review and source behavior
Next-update times derive from current-version receipts or the outreach due time.
Overdue work, missing approval, delivery issues and uncertain receipts enter the
EOC review queue. Recorded distributions retain exact-version references.
While the PIO view is open, county applicability checks occur initially and every
five minutes while visible. One failed feed is one failure notice; changed or
missing recorded zones create per-outreach applicability cues. No source change
automatically alters approved text or a county evacuation status.
Source IDs are OID + name + a SHA-256 representation of name/status/geometry;
the county layer has no GlobalID or individual issue/update fields. Query time is
not an order issue time. Geometry reordering may warrant a conservative review
without representing a real boundary change. OID/name reuse cannot move an
outreach history to a different zone; create a new record when identity changes.

The approximate population within a selected zone is a F.R.O.G. calculation, not
a zone total copied from the original county viewer: block population times its
geodetic polygon-area share inside the zone. Boundary blocks assume uniform
population, including water. Estimates may be poor for large rural blocks. These
are 2020 baseline residents, not current occupancy, evacuees or shelter demand.
Newer Living Atlas ACS tract values and 90% MOEs are separate. They are never
allocated to individual zones, buildings or persons. Census language categories
do not confirm individual preference; age/disability do not establish support
needs. Community partners and the county must confirm operational applicability.
