# Pine Flat / Kings Area Watch — 0.77.0

Purpose: help operators inspect the reservoir reporting point and near-dam gauge without working through a layer list. This refines the existing operational renderer and Calcite shell; it does not replace the engine or change data collectors, review thresholds, alert counts, source freshness or briefing snapshots.

Selection fits valid source points with a brief 900 ms transition after the evidence layout settles. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Latest selection wins across deferred initialization; closing the pane or changing workspaces invalidates its pending request. Source locations get white selection rings, not a connected route or inferred flood boundary. Other watchpoint names recede and other watchpoint symbols dim; hazard colors, utility features and countywide counts are preserved. Closing the evidence pane, changing workspaces or Fit workspace clears the selection treatment.

The pane leads with the review cue, requested verification, Focus area / View in 3D, and release, pool, rainfall and downstream stage values. Observations retain their timestamps, units and missing/stale labels. Sources, assignments, nearby roads/facilities, briefing context and technical tools use disclosures. The PFQC1 provisional / near-dam / no-travel-time qualification remains visible. Nearby records remain proximity context, not predicted exposure. Approved content and private permissions are untouched.

Verification: regression suite and deployment checks; interactive selection and map/evidence inspection; optional 3D and responsive layout checks. No performance improvement is asserted without measurement. Rollback is redeployment of 0.76.0; no schema or data migration.
